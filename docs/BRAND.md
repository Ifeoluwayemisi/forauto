FORauto — Brand Direction v0.1
1. Brand Core
Brand name
FORauto

Category: Automotive Services & Vehicle Solutions

The name is intentionally broad enough to cover the full business rather than locking it into only mechanical repairs or rewiring.

Brand essence

Experienced hands. Modern service.

The brand should combine two things:

20+ years of practical automotive experience

with

a cleaner, more convenient modern customer experience.

That contrast is the whole point.

2. Brand Personality

FORauto should feel:

Trait	How it should appear
Experienced	Confident, knowledgeable
Professional	Clean, structured
Practical	Straightforward language
Human	Real people and real work
Technical	Precise details
Trustworthy	Evidence over hype
Modern	Contemporary interface
Established	20+ years of experience
What FORauto is NOT
Loud
Cheap-looking
Overly corporate
"Street mechanic" aesthetic
Luxury-for-the-sake-of-luxury
Full of empty marketing claims
Dependent on stock photography
3. Brand Positioning

The key positioning is:

A trusted automotive workshop bringing decades of hands-on experience into a modern customer experience.

We're not pretending FORauto is a giant automotive corporation.

We're also not presenting it as:

"My dad fixes cars."

😂

It's an established business with real expertise that now has a proper digital identity.

4. Messaging Strategy

There are three layers.

Primary message

20+ years of automotive experience.

Functional message

Mechanical, electrical, diagnostics and vehicle services.

Emotional message

Confidence that your vehicle is in experienced hands.

These three should repeat throughout the website without sounding repetitive.

5. Tagline Direction

I'm not locking one final tagline yet because we should test the brand visually first.

But these are the strongest directions:

Direction A — Experience

Driven by experience.

Short. Memorable. Automotive without being cheesy.

Direction B — Craft

Built on experience. Driven by workmanship.

More premium and descriptive.

Direction C — Confidence

Experience you can drive with.

More consumer-facing.

Direction D — Straightforward

Know the problem. Fix the car.

More technical and conversational.

Direction E — Broad brand

Automotive expertise. Done right.

Simple and flexible.

For now, I'd keep:

Driven by experience.

as the working tagline.

Not final.

6. Brand Voice

FORauto speaks like an experienced technician who doesn't need to shout to prove they're good.

Confident

We'll diagnose the problem before recommending a repair.

Not:

"WE ARE THE #1 BEST AUTO REPAIR COMPANY!!!"

Clear

Tell us what's happening with your vehicle.

Not:

"Submit your automotive service requirements."

Human

Car refusing to start? Let's figure it out.

Technical when necessary

Electrical fault tracing and vehicle rewiring.

But immediately explain what that means for an ordinary driver.

9. Color Direction

I want FORauto to avoid the standard "mechanic red + black" combination.

Base

Obsidian / near-black

For:

Navigation
Hero sections
Footer
Strong contrast areas
Neutral

Warm white / stone

For:

Main backgrounds
Cards
Content areas
Accent

We need one recognizable accent.

Possible directions:

Electric amber

Feels mechanical/electrical.

Signal orange

Feels automotive and energetic.

Industrial blue

Feels technical and trustworthy.

Lime/electric green

Feels modern and electrical.

I'm leaning toward an amber/orange accent, but we're not locking it yet.

10. Color Philosophy

Whatever accent we choose:

80%

Neutral foundation.

15%

Dark/strong contrast.

5%

Brand accent.

The accent should feel like a signal, not wallpaper.

So no:

🟧🟧🟧🟧🟧 EVERYTHING IS ORANGE 🟧🟧🟧🟧🟧

😂

Buttons, highlights, status indicators and small details should carry it.

11. Typography

The typography needs to communicate precision.

Display

I'd explore:

Manrope / Geist / Inter Tight

Strong enough for huge headlines.

Body

Inter / Geist

Very readable on mobile.

Technical metadata

We can use a slightly tighter/monospaced treatment sparingly for things like:

FORAUTO / SERVICE 024
DIAGNOSTICS / 2026

That gives the interface a subtle engineering feel.

13. Photography Direction

This is probably more important than the logo.

Hero

A dramatic but authentic workshop/car image.

Not a random Lamborghini photographed in Dubai. 😭

Ideally:

Dad / technician actually working on a vehicle.

Secondary photography

Close-up shots:

Wiring
Engine components
Tools
Diagnostic equipment
Hands working
Mechanical components

These can create a beautiful editorial aesthetic.

14. Image Treatment

Photography should feel:

Documentary + premium.

Not overly edited.

We can use:

Dark crops
Strong contrast
Close framing
Depth
Environmental shots
Detail shots

And occasionally overlay technical information.

Example:

TOYOTA CAMRY
ELECTRICAL DIAGNOSTICS

FAULT IDENTIFIED
✓

That makes the work itself part of the visual language.

15. UI Personality

The interface should have:

Large typography

Big, confident headlines.

Cards

Used where information needs grouping.

Rounded corners

Moderate, not excessively bubbly.

Borders

Subtle technical borders.

Motion

Smooth and restrained.

For example:

A service card could slightly shift on hover.

A gallery image could reveal:

Electrical Diagnosis →

No crazy 3D car flying across the screen.

16. Motion Language

Motion should communicate movement and precision.

Good:

Fade/slide reveals
Image transitions
Hover states
Scroll-linked subtle movement
Number counters
Smooth page transitions
Expandable service details

Avoid:

Constant bouncing
Excessive parallax
Loading animations everywhere
Huge cursor gimmicks
Motion that delays access to content
Principle

Motion should make the interface feel alive, not make the user wait for it to finish performing.

20. Mobile-first

This is non-negotiable.

A huge percentage of local customers will likely encounter FORauto through:

WhatsApp → phone → mobile browser.

So the mobile version isn't a compressed desktop website.

It is its own first-class experience.

Persistent bottom CTA could eventually be:

┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ 💬 WhatsApp │ 📞 Call       │
└─────────────────────────────┘

Very practical.

21. Brand Rules
DO
Use real work
Be specific
Show experience
Explain technical things simply
Make contact easy
Use confident language
Prioritize mobile
Let the work prove the claim
DON'T
Invent certifications
Claim "best mechanic in Lagos"
Use fake testimonials
Invent years of experience beyond what we know
Use fake workshop photography once real photos exist
Overpromise repair outcomes
Make everything about discounts
Stuff pages with SEO keywords
22. The Core Brand Statement

If someone asks:

"What is FORauto?"

Our answer should be:

FORauto is an experienced automotive services business offering mechanical, electrical, diagnostic and vehicle-related services, backed by more than 20 years of hands-on experience.

Clean.

No corporate poetry required. 😂
