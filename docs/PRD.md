FORauto — Product Blueprint & PRD

Document status: Draft v0.1
Product type: Automotive services platform + business website
Initial market: Lagos, Nigeria
Primary service area: Oniru / Victoria Island
Business experience: 20+ years
Primary operator: Founder/owner + technicians/workmen
Primary channels: Phone, WhatsApp, referrals, physical workshop

1. Product Vision
FORauto
Automotive Services & Vehicle Solutions

FORauto is a modern digital platform for an established automotive service business with over 20 years of hands-on experience.

The platform will allow customers to:

Discover FORauto
Understand its services
View previous work
Request automotive assistance
Submit vehicle information
Contact the workshop
Request appointments
Request vehicle inspections
Enquire about vehicle sales assistance

Behind the public-facing website, FORauto will provide an internal system for managing customers, vehicles, service requests, appointments, jobs, technicians and vehicle-related services.

Core idea

Bring a modern digital experience to a traditional automotive business without losing the trust and human relationship that makes the business work.

2. The Problem

Traditional automotive businesses often depend heavily on:

Word of mouth
Phone calls
WhatsApp
Physical visibility
Returning customers
Personal relationships

Those channels are valuable, but information becomes scattered.

A customer may know the mechanic but not:

What services are available
Where exactly the workshop is
How to request a service
Whether the business handles their vehicle
What previous work has been done
How to schedule a visit

Meanwhile, the business may manage customers and jobs informally through conversations, memory and paper/phone records.

FORauto's digital platform addresses this gap.

3. Product Goals
Primary goals

G1 — Establish a professional digital presence

Make FORauto discoverable and credible online.

G2 — Generate customer enquiries

Make it extremely easy for potential customers to contact the business.

G3 — Simplify service requests

Allow customers to explain their vehicle problem before arriving.

G4 — Organize business operations

Give the owner a centralized view of customers, vehicles, jobs and requests.

G5 — Create a foundation for expansion

Allow FORauto to eventually support vehicle inspections, listings and other automotive services.

4. Non-Goals

For the first release, FORauto will not attempt to become:

Uber for mechanics
A full vehicle marketplace like Jiji
A parts marketplace
A roadside-assistance network
An insurance platform
A vehicle financing platform
A fleet-management SaaS
A multi-workshop marketplace

Those could be future opportunities.

We're building FORauto, not "Automotive Super App 3000." 😂

29. Key Product Principle

There's one principle I want us to keep throughout the entire build:

Digital should make the existing business better, not force the business to behave like a tech startup.

Your dad already has customers.

He already uses WhatsApp.

He already receives referrals.

He already has technicians.

He already knows how to repair cars.

We're building technology around what works, then improving the weak points.

That's the product.

One important correction before we freeze this

I'm intentionally treating some things as proposed capabilities, not confirmed existing services — particularly vehicle inspection workflows, digital service history, online vehicle listings, etc.

That doesn't mean we remove them. It means the PRD distinguishes:

Existing business capability → what FORauto already does.

Digital capability → what we're building to support it.

Future business capability → opportunities the platform could enable.

That keeps our requirements honest.
