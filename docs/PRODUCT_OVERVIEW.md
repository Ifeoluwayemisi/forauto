Product

FORauto

Category: Automotive Services & Vehicle Solutions
Location: Oniru, Victoria Island, Lagos
Experience: 20+ years
Primary users: Vehicle owners, prospective buyers, vehicle sellers, business owner, technicians

One-line description

FORauto is a modern digital platform for a long-established automotive service business, connecting vehicle owners with experienced automotive repair, electrical, diagnostic, inspection, and vehicle-related services.

Product vision

Make trusted automotive expertise easier to discover, access, and manage through a modern digital experience.

Brand promise

Experienced hands. Practical solutions.

The brand should communicate that FORauto is built on real-world automotive experience rather than simply presenting itself as another online auto-service company.

2. Product pillars

Everything we build should fall into one of these five pillars.

01 — Expertise

20+ years of hands-on automotive experience.

02 — Service

Mechanical, electrical, rewiring, diagnostics and general automotive work.

03 — Trust

Real people, real workshop, real work.

04 — Convenience

Customers can discover, enquire, request help and communicate without unnecessary friction.

05 — Continuity

The platform gradually creates an organized history of customers, vehicles and work performed.

3. The customer experience

The ideal journey is ridiculously simple:

10. Design Principle

Here's a rule I want us to put directly into DESIGN.md later:

Photography proves. Typography communicates. Interface guides. Motion supports.

Meaning:

We shouldn't rely on animations to make the site impressive.

The actual workshop and actual work are the hero.

That's what makes FORauto different from a fake concept project.

11. Brand Voice

FORauto shouldn't sound like a bank.

Instead of:

"We provide comprehensive automotive solutions tailored to meet the diverse needs of our esteemed clientele."

🤢

Use:

Your car has a problem. Let's find it.

Instead of:

"Our highly trained professionals utilize cutting-edge technology..."

Use:

We diagnose the problem before we start replacing parts.

Instead of:

"Contact us today to experience unparalleled service excellence."

Use:

Tell us what's wrong. We'll take it from there.

Simple. Confident. Human.
